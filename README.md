# BAR Maps

Maps for [Beyond All Reason](https://www.beyondallreason.info/).

## Simmering Ravine 1.5

`simmering_ravine_1.5.sd7`: a 14 × 20 map, mirrored top to bottom (Team A north, Team B south). It is built on All That Simmers by Nikuksis, in its textures and colours.

- **Cliffs** 450 high beside each base, with switchbacks of stone stairs cut into them. Bots and amphibious units climb them; tanks, the Thor and hovercraft can't.
- **The central route** is closed at each side by a destructible door in a stone rampart. The door is the only way down for vehicles and hovercraft.
- **The rampart** stops every unit but spiders and aircraft, and has a flat top to build turrets on. A commander's D-Gun flattens a stretch of it.
- **The river** is crossed by two stone bridges and a gravel ford.
- **Metal:** 54 spots, 62.4 metal/s a side, and 4 geothermal vents.

### Install

Copy `simmering_ravine_1.5.sd7` into BAR's `maps` folder (usually `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Beyond-All-Reason\data\maps`).

BAR's lobby only shows pictures for maps it already knows. To see this map's picture on the setup screen, also copy this repo's `LuaMenu` folder into BAR's data folder (the one that holds `maps`), then restart the lobby. That adds `LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps\Simmering_Ravine_1.5.jpg`. Each player's lobby reads its own data folder, so other players see the picture only if they copy it too.

## Five Rivers 1.0

`five_rivers_1.0.sd7`: a 28 × 28 map for five kings and their peasants, 20 players (5 teams of 4). Every fifth of it is the same, turned about the centre. Its plants, detail textures and gadgets are All That Simmers' by Nikuksis.

- **Each king** holds an oval cliff-top hall on the rim. A graded ramp faces the king's peasants; bots-only zigzag switchbacks climb the other three sides. The cliffs stop everything but spiders.
- **Five rivers** run out from a lake between the realms, each crossed by a stone bridge and a gravel ford. A bridge from each realm crosses the lake to the isle.
- **Metal:** 75 spots, 24.3 metal/s a team, and 11 geothermal vents: one on each hall, one in each realm, one on the isle.
- **The Bloom planet:** a glowing night world of teal moss, cyan-lit rivers, giant trees, mushrooms and crystals.

### Install

The map is 112 MB, over GitHub's limit for a file kept in a repository, so it is on this repo's release instead: download [five_rivers_1.0.sd7](https://github.com/danielkopko/BarMaps/releases/download/five-rivers-1.0/five_rivers_1.0.sd7) and copy it into BAR's `maps` folder.

Its lobby picture is in this repo's `LuaMenu` folder too (`LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps\Five_Rivers_1.0.jpg`, also on the release): copy it in as for Simmering Ravine above.

## Star Fort 1.0

`star_fort_1.0.sd7`: a 28 × 28 map for five kings and their peasants, 20 players (5 teams of 4). Every fifth of it is the same, turned about the centre. Its palms, detail textures and gadgets are All That Simmers' by Nikuksis.

- **One star fort holds all five kings**, each on a raised bastion: a flagstone court behind earth ramparts, round a spire in the middle.
- **A ramp** climbs in at each bastion's point, facing its peasants, for every unit. **Bots-only zigzags** cut up both flanks. There are no doors: the ramparts are earth, flat on top, so you can build on them.
- **The peasants** hold five realms of dunes outside the fort, parted by sandstone ridges with a bots-only pass over each.
- **Metal:** 70 spots, 27.2 metal/s a team, and 10 geothermal vents: one in each bastion, one in each realm.
- **The Dune planet:** golden dunes and sandstone under a deep blue sky, with palm oases at the ramps' feet, desert rocks, and half-buried wrecks.

### Install

The map is on this repo's release: download [star_fort_1.0.sd7](https://github.com/danielkopko/BarMaps/releases/download/star-fort-1.0/star_fort_1.0.sd7) and copy it into BAR's `maps` folder. Its lobby picture is `LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps\Star_Fort_1.0.jpg` (also on the release): copy it in as for Simmering Ravine above.

## Edge Keeps 1.0

`edge_keeps_1.0.sd7`: a 24 × 24 map for four kings and their peasants, 16 players (4 teams of 4). Every quarter of it is the same, turned about the centre. Its detail textures and gadgets are All That Simmers' by Nikuksis; the firs and birches are from Crimson Bay.

- **Each king holds a keep**, a granite cliff-top jutting from the middle of an edge. A ramp climbs it from the loch side for every unit, and bots-only zigzags climb both its flanks.
- **The peasants** hold the corner beside their keep, under the next king's cliff, so two kings look down on every corner.
- **A loch** with an isle lies in the middle, crossed by stone causeways.
- **Metal:** 56 spots, 28.4 metal/s a team, and 9 geothermal vents: one on each keep, one in each corner, one on the isle.
- **The Heath planet:** a highland moor of rolling heather and moor grass, peat hags, pinewoods and birch copses, granite tors and crags, under a grey, misty sky.

### Install

Download [edge_keeps_1.0.sd7](https://github.com/danielkopko/BarMaps/releases/download/edge-keeps-1.0/edge_keeps_1.0.sd7) from this repo's release and copy it into BAR's `maps` folder. Its lobby picture is `LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps\Edge_Keeps_1.0.jpg` (also on the release): copy it in as for Simmering Ravine above.

## Corner Fortresses 1.0

`corner_fortresses_1.0.sd7`: a 24 × 24 map for four kings and their peasants, 16 players (4 teams of 4). Every quarter of it is the same, turned about the centre. Its detail textures and gadgets are All That Simmers' by Nikuksis; the crates and trucks are from Industrial Revolution.

- **Each king holds a corner fortress**, a raised, paved yard behind earth ramparts. A ramp climbs through its gate for every unit, and bots-only zigzags cut through both inner ramparts. There are no doors: the ramparts are earth, flat on top, so you can build on them.
- **All twelve peasants** live in the middle, round a brick market square. Acid lakes part the fortresses, and they hurt anything in them.
- **Metal:** 64 spots, 32.8 metal/s a team, about 9,100 metal a team to reclaim in wrecks and rubble, and 9 geothermal vents.
- **The Forge planet:** a derelict industrial estate of cracked concrete lots and weedy waste ground, ruined works, container yards and trucks, under a brown smog sky.

### Install

Download [corner_fortresses_1.0.sd7](https://github.com/danielkopko/BarMaps/releases/download/corner-fortresses-1.0/corner_fortresses_1.0.sd7) from this repo's release and copy it into BAR's `maps` folder. Its lobby picture is `LuaMenu\Images\Minimaps\Corner_Fortresses_1.0.jpg` (also on the release): copy it in as for Simmering Ravine above.
